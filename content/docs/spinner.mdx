## 1\. What a Spinner Actually Is
A **spinner** (loading indicator) is a visual signal that a process is running but its duration is unknown. It's the _indeterminate_ counterpart to a Progress Bar (which shows _how far_ along you are).

The distinction matters:

**Spinner (this doc):** indeterminate. "Something is loading, I can't tell you when." Circular rotation, pulsing dots, bouncing bars.
**Progress Bar:** determinate. "You're 45% done." A filling bar with a known endpoint.
**Skeleton:** structural placeholder. "Here's what the content will look like when it arrives." Shows the _shape_ of the result.

All three are loading patterns but each carries different information. A spinner says "wait"; a progress bar says "wait X more"; a skeleton says "this is coming."

---

## 2\. Why It Matters
**Perceived performance.** A spinner doesn't make the load faster, but it prevents the user from thinking the app is broken. The absence of a spinner (a frozen screen) feels like a crash. Its presence says "I heard you, working on it."

**Trust maintenance.** Users will wait 3-10 seconds with a spinner before abandoning. Without one, they bail at 1-2 seconds. The spinner buys you time.

**Accessibility.** Screen-reader users need the equivalent signal: `aria-busy="true"` on the region, or an `aria-live` announcement ("Loading"). A purely visual spinner is invisible to them.

---

## 3\. Anatomy
**Animated element:** the visual moving part (rotating arc, pulsing dots, bouncing bars).
**Track (optional):** a static circle/bar behind the animated element showing the full path.
**Label (optional):** text like "Loading..." beneath or beside. Helpful for long waits and always good for accessibility.
**Container/region:** the area being loaded, marked with `aria-busy="true"` while the spinner is active.

---

## 4\. Sizes / Scale

| Token | Diameter | Stroke | Use |
| ---| ---| ---| --- |
| XS | 16px | 2px | Inline in buttons, inputs |
| S | 20px | 2.5px | Table cells, small cards |
| M | 24px | 3px | Default standalone |
| L | 32px | 3.5px | Section-level loading |
| XL | 48px | 4px | Full-page/centered loading |
| XXL | 64px | 5px | Initial app load, splash |

The spinner inside a button replaces the label text and inherits the button's size constraints.

---

## 5\. States
**Spinning (active):** the default. Continuously animating.
**Appear:** fade/scale in when loading begins. Delay 200-400ms before showing to avoid flash for fast loads.
**Disappear:** fade out when loading completes. Transition to content.
**Hidden:** `display: none` or unmounted. The region's `aria-busy` flips to `false`.

The single most important timing rule: **never show a spinner for loads under 200ms.** A flash of spinner for an instant load feels janky. Use a minimum delay before mounting.

---

## 6\. Types / Variants
**Circular (ring):** the classic. A rotating arc on a circular track. Universal recognition.
**Dots (pulse):** 3-4 dots that pulse in sequence. Used in chat ("typing...") and messaging.
**Bar (linear indeterminate):** a bar that slides back and forth along a track. Material's linear progress in indeterminate mode.
**Bounce:** elements that bounce vertically in sequence. Playful.
**Fade:** segments of a circle that fade in sequence (iOS classic style).
**Orbital:** multiple elements rotating around a center. Premium/complex.

Functional variants:
**Inline:** inside a button, input, or table cell.
**Overlay:** centered over a dimmed content area.
**Full-page:** centered in the viewport with a backdrop.
**Skeleton companion:** spinner shown briefly before skeleton appears.

---

## 7\. When to Use (and When Not To)
**Use a spinner when:**
*   The operation is indeterminate (you don't know how long)
*   The wait is 1-10 seconds
*   The content shape isn't known (can't use skeleton)
*   Inside a button or small container where skeleton doesn't fit

**Use something else when:**
*   You know the progress → Progress Bar
*   You know the content shape → Skeleton
*   The load is < 200ms → show nothing (avoid spinner flash)
*   The load is > 10s → add a progress bar or status text; spinner alone loses trust
*   The whole page is loading → consider skeleton or progressive rendering

---

## 8\. Across Design Systems
**Material:** `CircularProgress` (indeterminate mode) and `LinearProgress`. Determinate and indeterminate share the same component with a `variant` prop.
**Apple HIG:** `UIActivityIndicatorView` (the classic gray fan), `ProgressView` with no value. iOS 26 adds Liquid Glass treatment.
**Fluent:** `Spinner` component with sizes and label position options.
**Ant Design:** `<Spin>` wraps content and overlays a spinner. `tip` prop for the label.
**Chakra:** `<Spinner>` with size, color, speed, emptyColor props.
**shadcn/Radix:** no built-in spinner; community snippets use SVG animation.

import { HTMLAttributes } from "react";

type SpinnerType = "circular" | "dots" | "bar";
type SpinnerSize = "xs" | "sm" | "md" | "lg" | "xl";

interface SpinnerProps extends HTMLAttributes<HTMLDivElement> {
  type?: SpinnerType;

  color?: string;

  const px = SIZES[size];

  return (
    <div className={`spinner spinner--${size} ${className}`} role="status" aria-label={label}
         style={color ? { '--spinner-color': color } as React.CSSProperties : undefined} {...rest}>
      {type === "circular" && (
        <svg viewBox="0 0 24 24" width={px} height={px} className="spinner__svg">
          <circle className="spinner__track" cx="12" cy="12" r="10" fill="none" strokeWidth="3" />
          <circle className="spinner__arc" cx="12" cy="12" r="10" fill="none" strokeWidth="3"
                  strokeLinecap="round" strokeDasharray="60 200" />
        </svg>
      )}
      {type === "dots" && (
        <div className="spinner-dots">

          <span className="spinner-dots__dot" />

        <div className="spinner-bar"><div className="spinner-bar__fill" /></div>
      )}
      {showLabel && <span className="spinner__label">{label}</span>}

      <AsyncDataComponent />
    </Suspense>
  );
}

// Delayed spinner (avoid flash for fast loads):
"use client";
import { useState, useEffect } from "react";

export function DelayedSpinner({ delay = 200, ...props }: SpinnerProps & { delay?: number }) {
  const [show, setShow] = useState(false);
  useEffect(() => {
    const timer = setTimeout(() => setShow(true), delay);
    return () => clearTimeout(timer);
  }, [delay]);
  if (!show) return null;
  return <Spinner {...props} />;
}

import SwiftUI

struct SpinnerView: View {
    enum SpinnerType { case circular, dots, bar }
    enum SpinnerSize: CGFloat { case xs = 16, sm = 20, md = 24, lg = 32, xl = 48 }

    var type: SpinnerType = .circular
    var size: SpinnerSize = .md
    var label: String = "Loading"
    var showLabel: Bool = false
    var color: Color = .purple

    @State private var isAnimating = false

    var body: some View {
        VStack(spacing: 8) {
            switch type {
            case .circular:
                Circle()
                    .trim(from: 0.0, to: 0.7)
                    .stroke(color, style: StrokeStyle(lineWidth: 3, lineCap: .round))
                    .frame(width: size.rawValue, height: size.rawValue)
                    .rotationEffect(.degrees(isAnimating ? 360 : 0))
                    .animation(.linear(duration: 1.4).repeatForever(autoreverses: false), value: isAnimating)

            case .dots:
                HStack(spacing: 4) {
                    ForEach(0..<3) { index in
                        Circle()
                            .fill(color)
                            .frame(width: size.rawValue / 4, height: size.rawValue / 4)
                            .scaleEffect(isAnimating ? 1.0 : 0.5)
                            .opacity(isAnimating ? 1.0 : 0.4)
                            .animation(
                                .easeInOut(duration: 0.6)
                                .repeatForever()
                                .delay(Double(index) * 0.15),
                                value: isAnimating
                            )
                    }
                }

            case .bar:
                GeometryReader { geo in
                    RoundedRectangle(cornerRadius: 2)
                        .fill(color.opacity(0.2))
                        .frame(height: 4)
                        .overlay(alignment: .leading) {
                            RoundedRectangle(cornerRadius: 2)
                                .fill(color)
                                .frame(width: geo.size.width * 0.4)
                                .offset(x: isAnimating ? geo.size.width : -geo.size.width * 0.4)
                                .animation(.easeInOut(duration: 1.6).repeatForever(autoreverses: false), value: isAnimating)
                        }
                }
                .frame(width: 100, height: 4)
            }

            if showLabel {
                Text(label)
                    .font(.caption)
                    .foregroundColor(.secondary)
            }
        }
        .accessibilityElement(children: .ignore)
        .accessibilityLabel(label)
        .accessibilityAddTraits(.updatesFrequently)
        .onAppear { isAnimating = true }
    }
}

  final Color color;

  State<_DotsSpinner> createState() => _DotsSpinnerState();

class _DotsSpinnerState extends State<_DotsSpinner> with TickerProviderStateMixin {
  late final List<AnimationController> _controllers;

  @override
  void initState() {
    super.initState();
    _controllers = List.generate(3, (i) =>
      AnimationController(vsync: this, duration: const Duration(milliseconds: 600))
        ..repeat(reverse: true)
        ..addStatusListener((_) {})
    );
    for (var i = 0; i < 3; i++) {
      Future.delayed(Duration(milliseconds: i * 150), () {
        if (mounted) _controllers[i].repeat(reverse: true);
      });
    }
  }

## 10\. Accessibility
**`role="status"`** on the spinner container announces to screen readers politely. Don't use `role="alert"` (that's assertive/interruptive).

**`aria-busy="true"`** on the region being loaded tells AT that content is not yet ready. Flip to `false` when done.

**Visible or sr-only label:** "Loading" text either visible below the spinner or as `aria-label`. The animation alone is invisible to screen readers.

**`prefers-reduced-motion`****\*\*\*\*:** slow the rotation dramatically or switch to a static pulsing opacity. Never remove the indicator entirely.

**Minimum display time:** show for at least 500ms even if the load finishes faster, to avoid a confusing flash of spinner + content.

---

## 11\. Innovative / Emerging Ideas
*   **Branded spinners:** custom SVG animations that reinforce brand identity
*   **Progress-aware spinners:** start indeterminate, switch to determinate as data arrives
*   **AI streaming indicators:** pulsing dots for LLM generation, transitioning to skeleton as structure emerges
*   **Haptic feedback:** on mobile, a subtle vibration when loading completes

---

## 12\. Conversion / UX Killers
*   **Spinner for sub-200ms loads:** a flash of spinner makes fast things feel slow
*   **No minimum display:** spinner appears for 50ms and vanishes, feels glitchy
*   **Spinner without context:** users don't know _what_ is loading. Add a label.
*   **Infinite spinner (no timeout):** if the request fails silently, the spinner spins forever. Always have a timeout + error state.
*   **Full-page spinner blocking interaction:** users can't navigate away or cancel
*   **Animation janking the main thread:** complex JS animations during a heavy load

---

## 13\. Advanced Patterns
**Delayed appearance:** mount the spinner with `opacity: 0`, transition to visible after 200-400ms. If loading completes before the delay, the spinner never shows.

**Skeleton → Spinner fallback:** show skeleton immediately; if load exceeds 5s, overlay a spinner to signal "still working" since a frozen skeleton looks broken.

**Button loading state:** replace label with spinner, disable the button, keep the button width stable (min-width or fixed width) to prevent layout shift.

---

## 14\. Performance & Bundle Cost
*   **CSS animation over JS.** CSS `@keyframes` runs on the compositor thread and won't jank during heavy main-thread work. requestAnimationFrame-driven spinners will freeze during the exact moments you need them most.
*   **SVG over GIF.** SVGs are resolution-independent, stylable, and tiny. GIFs are fixed-size, can't be themed, and are often 5-20KB.
*   **Avoid layout thrash on mount/unmount.** Reserve the spinner's space in layout so content doesn't jump.

---

## 15\. Security
Minimal security surface. The only concern: if the spinner's `aria-label` is dynamically generated from server content, sanitize it to prevent XSS.

---

## 16\. Senior-Level Checklist
*   `role="status"` on the spinner (polite announcement)
*   `aria-busy="true"` on the loading region
*   Visible or sr-only label ("Loading...")
*   `prefers-reduced-motion` honored (slow/pause animation)
*   Delayed appearance (no flash for fast loads, ≥200ms delay)
*   Minimum display time (≥500ms to avoid flicker)
*   Timeout + error state (never spin forever)
*   CSS animation, not JS (compositor thread)
*   Button loading state keeps stable width

---

## 17\. Visual Styles
**Flat:** simple arc rotation, solid color on light track.
**Material:** Material's signature stretch-and-rotate (dasharray animation). Tonal color roles.
**Glassmorphism:** translucent spinner over blurred content. Track barely visible.
**Liquid Glass:** refractive arc with specular highlights. Gorgeous but ensure the spinner remains visible on any background.
**Neumorphism:** spinner appears inset into the soft surface. Track is the "groove."
**Skeuomorphism:** the classic iOS "fan" with radial fading segments.
**Neo-Brutalism:** thick black arc, hard edges, no antialiasing.
**Claymorphism:** puffy, rounded stroke ends, bouncy easing.
**Aurora/Gradient:** the arc itself is an animated gradient. Honor reduced-motion.
**Minimal/Swiss:** thin, single-weight arc. No track. Just the moving line.
**UJG Brand:** Eminence arc on Goldenrod track, warm glow pulse. The house default.
